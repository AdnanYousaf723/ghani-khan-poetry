Roman Pashto
Yawa wraz, yao sahra ke, pa shkar rawan wum
Yao gulab me walar walido, pa khanda ke khkulo janan
Za khafa, war nazdik laaram, ma we: ah, zama pa shan
Ta hum gul ye bad-naseeba, da zulfano da janan
Na ba cha naazuke gute, narm makh ta ba de yosi
Na ba de sra shonde shkol kri, da yaar naazuk labaan
Hagha gharay muskay sho, waye: khana, khafagan ma kawa
Za ba da sahra warna na kram, da Iran pa gulistan
Delta za yao aw yakta yam, halta zar de zama pa shan
Char-chapera spera khawre, za yawaze yam roshan
Delta pa de tor registan ke, za da noor lamba yam
Da khkula chup naghma yam, da la-makan karishma yam
Sta pa bagh ke pa zargono, gulab de zama pa shan
Yao be-noma sor daryab ke, yao be-noma khakay rawan
Ta hum pa khpal sahra ke, khafa ma sha zama wrora
Akhir ba de deedan la, rashi khpal Ghani Khan
English
In a desert, once, on a hunt did I find,
With a radiant smile, a flower so fair;
Sadly, I approached and sighed, "Ah! Of my kind
Are you too — a hapless flower from a beloved's hair.
Frail fingers wouldn't take you to a soft face so close,
Nor would you be kissed by lips delicate and rose."
With a silent smile the flower replied, "Don't lose heart!
This desert I wouldn't give up for the gardens of Iran,
A solitary I am here while legions are there,
Amidst this cursed soil I stand apart.
In this gray desert, a flamboyant flame of divine light am I,
Beauty's silent song, a miracle from the sky.
In your garden, there are thousands of flowers like me —
A nameless droplet in a nameless sea.
You too, in your desert, don't feel forlorn,
To behold you at last shall come a sore Ghani Khan.

Roman Pashto
Za yao mast shan lewantob yam, da hawa pa as rawan yam

Da za sa la jor shway yam? na khabar yam, na pohegam

English
I am madness in raptures borne on an airy steed,
A hue of beloved eyes coloring up in dance.
Why, what am I made for, I neither know nor gather;
Now a mood, now melody, a voice that just rings on.
I am a flame descending to the heart's hidden cellars;
I am a jingling joy, a drunkenness in raptures.
In your veins a fire, I am a quivering flame,
A sparkling radiance, burning passion, yearning.
I don't exist; I'm wind, heaving joy on joy;
With tears in my cheer and sad, smiling eyes.
Speak up, madman! What makes you weep with me?
A mere illusive thought or an ever-unfolding grace;
A reckless airy steed rushing through reflections;
Or made of beat and jingle a prayer that is heard.

Roman Pashto
Ay da husn aw noor daryaba, ma la yao takay da noor ra
Stargay ra, da khanda ra; shonde ra, da suroor ra
Za de khpal wroki zargi la, yao nishan da yaar ghwaram
Ay da husn aw noor daryaba, ma la yao takay da noor ra
De tak-tor da gham mahal ke, yao bakhray da rana ra

De khpal swey baghche la, yawa wada da bahar ghwaram
Za na wayam jannat ra, za na wayam toor ra
Da de khob, da sa aw wakht, ishara da tabeer rakra
Ma la zra da badshahano, aw seena da faqeer ra
English
O river of beauty and radiance! Grant me a scintilla of light;
Grant me eyes full of laughter and lips full of delight.
For this minor heart of mine, I seek a beloved's souvenir;
O river of beauty and radiance! Grant me a scintilla of light.
For this pitch-dark sorrow's manor, a glowing grain to quell the night;
Grant me eyes full of laughter and lips full of delight.
For this rapture and its yearning, grant me a dear beloved's sight,
Indulge me with your greater love; grant me your gracious face's pride.
For this being's withered garden, I seek a covenant of spring,
I don't ask you to grant me heaven; I'm not seeking Sinai's height.
Let this dream's very breath and time point to its own interpretation,
Grant me the bosom of a fakir, and a heart with a shah's elation.

I cherish it now as its beauty is soon consumed.
An eternally full moon, an eternal sweet sixteen —
Eternal youth, a river of wine — is it a reward or hell?
I'd weep after this world, and yearn for the night's crescent,
And remember everyday, the thin mist of eventide.
Man seeks in each new palace a new beloved;
